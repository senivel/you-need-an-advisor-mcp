Perform a budget health check.

1. Read the accounts resource at `ynab://budgets/{budget_id}/accounts` to see current account balances.
2. Read the categories resource at `ynab://budgets/{budget_id}/categories` to see budget status.
3. Use the `manage_budgets` tool with action="get" for the budget-level summary.
4. Report on: total account balances, to-be-budgeted amount, overspent categories, and underfunded goals.
5. Suggest actionable next steps to improve budget health.
