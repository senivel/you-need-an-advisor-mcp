Review monthly spending for {month}.

1. Read the categories resource at `ynab://budgets/{budget_id}/categories` to understand the budget structure.
2. Use the `manage_months` tool with action="get" and month="{month}" to see the month summary.
3. Use the `manage_transactions` tool with action="list" and month="{month}" to see all transactions for the month.
4. Summarize spending by category, comparing budgeted amounts vs actual activity.
5. Highlight any categories that are over budget (negative balance).
6. Note the overall budget health: to-be-budgeted amount, total income vs total spending.
